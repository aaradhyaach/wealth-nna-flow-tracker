Method and caveats

Sources. Quarterly earnings releases, 10-Q/10-K filings and earnings-call transcripts for Morgan Stanley, Schwab and LPL Financial, through 3Q26 (LPL reports 3Q26 on Oct 30). Schwab figures are core NNA; LPL figures are organic NNA. Each firm defines NNA differently, so growth rates compare better than dollars.

Event adjustment. Where a firm quantified a one-off ("just over half associated with IPO-related flows"), the event share is estimated from that wording and removed to show the underlying run-rate.

Forecast. Models the ex-event growth rate rather than dollars, chooses among three simple methods by rolling-origin backtest (8 quarters), then converts back to dollars on the latest client assets. Event upside — IPO lock-up distributions, new corporate retirement mandates — is real but not forecastable from history and is left out of the base case.
